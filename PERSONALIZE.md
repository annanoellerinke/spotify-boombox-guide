# Make the boombox yours

Give your coding agent whatever you know. “I'm unsure—show me a few options” is a useful answer too. Ask it to work through the choices in small groups.

## Your name

- Name on the website: `[YOUR NAME]`
- Heading: `[YOUR NAME]’s Spotify`, or your own title.
- Name printed on the paper insert: `[YOUR NAME IN CAPITALS]`.
- A short note, if you want one: `[YOUR WORDS]`.

The included `owner` setting updates the page heading, browser title, and printed owner marks. A different heading format, tagline, or footer needs a small text edit by your agent. Longer names should be checked on a phone and in the insert's printed band.

## Your tapes

Start with three. The example is laid out for three tapes; adding more means adjusting the shelf and checking mobile spacing.

| Tape | Exact Spotify playlist link | Doodle | Optional color or design note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `[INSERT LINK]` | Star, heart, smile, or your idea | `[YOUR NOTE]` |
| 2 | `[INSERT LINK]` | Star, heart, smile, or your idea | `[YOUR NOTE]` |
| 3 | `[INSERT LINK]` | Star, heart, smile, or your idea | `[YOUR NOTE]` |

Choose which tape should be selected first. Your agent should get the real playlist title and song order from each playlist, then show you what it found. If the list is incomplete or unavailable, supply the missing tracks or choose an authorized data connection. Don't fill gaps with made-up songs.

The playlist title goes on the cassette, in the boombox, and on the insert. Your name goes in the owner branding. They are different pieces of text.

## What should it look like?

| Detail | Anna's version | Your choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boombox | Wide chrome body, raised handle, black speaker mesh | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Tapes | Cream shells, red and black labels, straight-on | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Background | Simple warm cream | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Paper insert | A/B columns, ruled paper, red and black printed bands | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Handwriting | Red marker, uneven rhythm, small doodles | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Heading | Bevan, the bold vintage serif | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |
| Lighting | Soft and even, clean materials, restrained shadows | `[YOUR CHOICE]` |

Attach reference pictures and say which detail each one is for. A reference is inspiration, not a playlist destination. Use artwork and fonts you have permission to use; the included font license files travel with the fonts.

If you want realistic objects, ask to approve the boombox, one cassette, and one paper insert before generating a whole layout. Keep the playlist lettering editable so changing a title doesn't require a new image.

## Feedback you can copy

> Save this version. Show me a separate variation with [CHANGE], keeping everything else the same.

> I like the cassette shape, but the shadow makes it look dirty. Keep the cream plastic and make the lighting more even.

> Show me five versions of just the heading so I can choose the font.

> The paper should look handwritten and playful, but every song still needs to be readable. Show me the longest title on a phone.

> Keep the official Spotify player easy to find. I want people to be able to listen even if the decorative controls don't start playback in their browser.
